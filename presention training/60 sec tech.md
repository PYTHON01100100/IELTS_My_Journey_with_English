## Video #1: What is Cloud Computing?

**Duration**: ~60 seconds  
**Link**: https://youtu.be/kzIAtk3d3Oo  
**Focus**: Clear explanation, relatable examples, engaging tone

### Script

Imagine you want to run a website.

The traditional way is to buy a server, set it up, maintain it, and hope it doesn't decide to ruin your weekend. 😅

With cloud computing, you don't need to own the physical server.

You simply rent computing resources from a cloud provider.

You can get servers, storage, databases, networking, and even AI services in just a few minutes.

And if your website suddenly becomes popular, you can scale up.

If everyone gets bored and leaves... well, you can scale down. 😂

That's basically cloud computing:

**Use what you need, when you need it, and pay for what you use.**

### Key Points
- ✅ Relatable analogy (buying vs. renting)
- ✅ Practical benefits (speed, scalability)
- ✅ Humor to keep audience engaged
- ✅ Clear takeaway message
