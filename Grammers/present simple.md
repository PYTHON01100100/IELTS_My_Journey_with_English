## Introduction

The **Present Simple** is one of the most important and frequently used tenses in English. It describes actions, states, and facts that are true in the present time. Whether you're learning English as a second language or teaching it to others, understanding Present Simple is fundamental to building strong communication skills.

---

## What is Present Simple?

The Present Simple tense is used to:
- Describe habits and routines
- State facts and general truths
- Express permanent situations
- Describe scheduled events in the future
- Give instructions or commands

### Definition
The Present Simple tense expresses an action that happens regularly, a state that is always true, or a fact that is universally accepted. It is the base form of the verb (infinitive without "to").

## Rules & Formation

### Rule 1: Affirmative Sentences (Positive)

**Structure: Subject + Verb + Object**

| Subject | Verb | Rest |
|---------|------|------|
| I, You, We, They | Play/Work/Eat | football/in an office/pizza |
| He, She, It | Plays/Works/Eats | football/in an office/pizza |

**Important**: When the subject is **he, she, or it** (singular third person), add **-s** or **-es** to the verb.

**Examples:**
- I play basketball every weekend.
- You work hard in your job.
- She studies English every day.
- It rains a lot in winter.
- They eat dinner at 6 PM.

### Rule 2: Negative Sentences (Negative)

**Structure: Subject + do/does + NOT + Verb**

| Subject | do/does | NOT | Verb |
|---------|---------|-----|------|
| I, You, We, They | do | not | play |
| He, She, It | does | not | play |

**Contraction:** 
- do not = don't
- does not = doesn't

**Examples:**
- I don't like pineapple on pizza.
- You don't speak French fluently.
- She doesn't work on Sundays.
- He doesn't know the answer.
- They don't live in London.

### Rule 3: Interrogative Sentences (Questions)

**Structure: do/does + Subject + Verb?**

| do/does | Subject | Verb | Rest |
|---------|---------|------|------|
| Do | I, you, we, they | play | football? |
| Does | he, she, it | play | football? |

**Examples:**
- Do you speak English?
- Does she like pizza?
- Do they work here?
- Does it snow in your country?
- Do we need to prepare anything?

### Rule 4: Verb Spelling Rules with -s/-es

When adding **-s** or **-es** to verbs, follow these rules:

**Rule 4a: Regular verbs + s**
- play → **plays**
- work → **works**
- want → **wants**

**Rule 4b: Verbs ending in -s, -ss, -x, -z, -ch, -sh add -es**
- pass → **passes**
- fix → **fixes**
- catch → **catches**
- wash → **washes**

**Rule 4c: Verbs ending in consonant + y, change y to i and add -es**
- study → **studies**
- carry → **carries**
- worry → **worries**

**Rule 4d: Verbs ending in vowel + y just add -s**
- play → **plays**
- enjoy → **enjoys**

## Examples

### 1. Habits and Routines
- I drink coffee every morning.
- She goes to the gym three times a week.
- They watch TV after dinner.
- He plays soccer on Saturdays.

### 2. Facts and General Truths
- Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.
- The Earth revolves around the Sun.
- Paris is the capital of France.
- Cats are independent animals.

### 3. Permanent Situations
- I live in New York.
- She works as a teacher.
- They own a restaurant.
- He has a small apartment.

### 4. Scheduled Future Events
- The train leaves at 9 AM tomorrow.
- The movie starts at 7 PM.
- My flight departs in two hours.
- The conference begins next Monday.

### 5. Instructions and Commands
- You mix the flour with sugar.
- First, you add the eggs.
- Then, you bake it for 30 minutes.

## Study Exercises

### Exercise 1: Fill in the Blanks (Choose -s or base form)

**Level: Beginner**

1. She _______ (work) at a bank.
2. I _______ (like) ice cream.
3. They _______ (play) tennis every week.
4. He _______ (study) mathematics.
5. We _______ (go) to the beach in summer.

**Answers:** 1. works  2. like  3. play  4. studies  5. go

---

### Exercise 2: Write the Negative Form

**Level: Beginner-Intermediate**

1. She likes coffee. → She _________________.
2. He works on Sundays. → He _________________.
3. They speak Spanish. → They _________________.
4. I drink milk. → I _________________.
5. We live in Paris. → We _________________.

**Answers:**
1. She doesn't like coffee.
2. He doesn't work on Sundays.
3. They don't speak Spanish.
4. I don't drink milk.
5. We don't live in Paris.

### Exercise 4: Multiple Choice

**Level: Intermediate**

Choose the correct answer:

1. She _______ English every day.
   - [ ] A. study
   - [ ] B. studies
   - [ ] C. are studying

2. Do you _______ in New York?
   - [ ] A. live
   - [ ] B. lives
   - [ ] C. are living

3. He _______ go to the office on Mondays.
   - [ ] A. don't
   - [ ] B. doesn't
   - [ ] C. not

4. _______ they play football?
   - [ ] A. Do
   - [ ] B. Does
   - [ ] C. Are

5. The train _______ at 8 AM.
   - [ ] A. leave
   - [ ] B. leaves
   - [ ] C. is leaving

**Answers:** 1-B, 2-A, 3-B, 4-A, 5-B

### Exercise 5: Complete the Dialogue

**Level: Intermediate-Advanced**

**Your task:** Fill in the blanks with the correct form of Present Simple.

---

**Dialogue: At a Coffee Shop**

**Customer**: Good morning! I _______ (want) a cappuccino, please.

**Barista**: Sure! _______ you (take) sugar?

**Customer**: No, I _______ (not/take) sugar. I _______ (prefer) it black.

**Barista**: Okay. _______ (Do/Does) this _______ (be) your first time here?

**Customer**: Yes, my friend _______ (recommend) this place. She _______ (come) here every week.

**Barista**: Great! We _______ (have) fresh pastries too. Our bakers _______ (make) them every morning at 6 AM.

**Customer**: That _______ (sound) wonderful! I'll take a croissant as well.

**Answers:**
1. want
2. Do
3. don't take
4. prefer
5. Does
6. be
7. recommends
8. comes
9. have
10. make
11. sounds

## Key Takeaways

✅ **Present Simple = Base Verb (or base + s/es for 3rd person singular)**

✅ **Use for: Habits, Facts, Permanent situations, Scheduled events**

✅ **Remember: He/She/It ALWAYS gets -s or -es**

✅ **Negatives: Use do/does + not + base verb**

✅ **Questions: Start with Do/Does?**
